Privacy Policy

PDF & Image Tools · Effective date: 18 November 2026

Short version: your PDFs and images are processed on your device and are never uploaded to us. We have no accounts and do not collect your name, email or files. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect your device's advertising identifier and related data to serve them (details below).

1. About this policy

This policy explains how the PDF & Image Tools mobile app for iOS and Android ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

2. Your files stay on your device

All PDF and image tools (merge, split, compress, resize, convert, scan, sign and similar) run locally on your phone. Files you open, scan or create are not uploaded to our servers or anyone else's, and we cannot see them. We do not operate a server that receives your content, and the app has no sign-in or account system.

3. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera - only to scan paper documents into a PDF. Pictures stay on your device.
  • Photo library - to let you choose photos to process, and to save finished images (iOS: add-only access to save).
  • Files / storage - to open documents you select and to save or share results. On Android the app may use read and write storage access for this purpose.
  • Internet - used only to load and show ads. Your files are not sent over the network.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings. Tools that need a declined permission will not work, but the rest of the app will.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use data from your device, such as:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, OS version, language) and app interaction data such as ad impressions and clicks
  • Diagnostic and performance data from the ads SDK

We do not receive this data and do not combine it with any information about you. Google's use of data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Your choices over ads and tracking

  • iOS: the app asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before using your device identifier for tracking. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EU, EEA and UK users: on first launch the app shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) so you can choose how your data is used for ads. You can change your choice when the form is offered again.
  • Android: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

6. Information we collect ourselves

We do not collect, store or sell personal information. We do not run our own analytics, and the app does not have user accounts. If you email us, we will see your email address and message, and use them only to reply.

7. Data retention and deletion

Files you create stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. Data held by Google for advertising is subject to Google's policies and your device's ad settings.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help.

9. Security

Because files are processed locally and not transmitted, they are protected by your device's own security. Ads are loaded over secure connections where the ad network supports it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed; material changes will be reflected in the app's store listing or this page.
